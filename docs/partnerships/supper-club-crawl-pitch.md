# Door County Supper Club Crawl
## Partnership Proposal

---

## The Concept

A guided, all-inclusive evening experience celebrating Wisconsin's beloved supper club tradition. Guests board a trolley and visit 3-4 supper clubs in one unforgettable night — cocktails at one, appetizers at another, entrée at a third, and dessert at the last.

**Tagline:** *"One night. Four clubs. A Wisconsin tradition."*

---

## Why This Works

### For Guests
- **Bucket-list experience** — Wisconsin supper clubs are a dying art; this celebrates the tradition
- **No driving** — enjoy old fashioneds without worrying about getting home
- **Curated evening** — we handle the reservations, the route, the timing
- **Social experience** — meet fellow food lovers, make it an event

### For Supper Clubs
- **Guaranteed covers on slow nights** — Tues/Wed/Thurs fill empty seats
- **New customer acquisition** — introduce tourists to YOUR club
- **No extra work** — we handle booking, payment, logistics
- **Premium positioning** — be part of an exclusive, curated experience
- **Cross-promotion** — guests discover clubs they wouldn't have found otherwise

### For Door County Trolley
- **New revenue stream** — premium evening experiences
- **Shoulder season business** — winter crawls when summer tours slow down
- **Exclusive partnership** — first trolley company to offer this in Door County
- **Brand elevation** — culinary tourism is high-value

### For Door County Visitor (Us)
- **Bookable experience** — direct revenue, not just advertising
- **Content goldmine** — photos, videos, reviews for marketing
- **Email list builder** — capture high-intent visitors
- **Premium positioning** — we're not just a directory, we're creating experiences

## The Experience

### Sample Itinerary: "The Classic Crawl"

| Time | Stop | Location | Experience |
|------|------|----------|------------|
| 5:30 PM | Pickup | Sturgeon Bay / Fish Creek | Board trolley, welcome drink |
| 6:00 PM | **Stop 1** | Nightingale Supper Club | Brandy old fashioneds & relish tray |
| 7:00 PM | **Stop 2** | Mr. G's | Appetizers (shrimp cocktail, cheese curds) |
| 8:00 PM | **Stop 3** | The English Inn | Main course (prime rib or fish fry) |
| 9:15 PM | **Stop 4** | Sister Bay Bowl | Dessert & ice cream drinks |
| 10:00 PM | Drop-off | Return to pickup points |

*Route and clubs rotate to ensure all partners get featured.*

## Operating Model

### Scheduling
- **Peak Season (Jun-Aug):** Thursday & Saturday nights
- **Shoulder Season (Sep-Oct, Apr-May):** Friday & Saturday
- **Winter (Nov-Mar):** Friday only — "Fireside Crawl" branding
- **Capacity:** 14-24 guests per crawl (depending on trolley)

### Club Rotation
- 6-8 participating clubs in the program
- Rotate which clubs are featured each week
- Ensures every partner gets equal exposure over the season
- Clubs can "buy" additional featured nights

### Booking & Logistics
- **Door County Visitor handles:**
  - Online booking & payment
  - Customer communication
  - Marketing & promotion
  - Reservation coordination with clubs
  
- **Door County Trolley handles:**
  - Vehicle & driver
  - Route logistics
  - On-board hospitality
  
- **Supper Clubs handle:**
  - Reserved seating (semi-private when possible)
  - Pre-set menu or limited options
  - Food & beverage execution
